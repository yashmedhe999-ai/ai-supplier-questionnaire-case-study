All names, documents, and statements below are invented for this portfolio.

**Question:** Does your organization require multi-factor authentication for all employees accessing production systems?

**Uploaded example passage:** `Example Access Policy, section 4.2: All employees must use multi-factor authentication when signing in to production systems. Privileged accounts require hardware-backed factors.`

**Evidence shown to user:** Example Access Policy, section 4.2, with a direct passage link.

**Supplier action:** Open the cited section, confirm the policy is current and applies to the full employee population, then accept or edit. The answer is not submitted automatically.

**Failure variant:** If the passage says only `administrators` use MFA, the proposed answer must **not** claim coverage for all employees. Route it to manual review and explain the scope mismatch.

**Product lesson:** Abbreviation matching can find a passage; source inspection establishes whether the passage actually answers the question.
